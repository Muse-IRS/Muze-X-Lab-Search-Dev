# Contributing

**Muze-X Lab Search & Dev** is a publication surface for versioned research snapshots. Contributions are welcome when they improve reproducibility, detect errors, supply counterexamples, strengthen provenance, or clarify scientific status without overstating certainty.

## Preferred contribution types

- reproducibility reports;
- counterexamples or failed replications;
- mathematical corrections;
- source/provenance corrections;
- alternative hypotheses with explicit discriminant tests;
- implementation corrections tied to a published ERP;
- documentation improvements that preserve the original claim boundary.

## Required discipline

A contribution that changes scientific meaning should identify:

1. the ERP identifier and exact version affected;
2. the statement being challenged or changed;
3. the evidence, derivation, test or counterexample;
4. the old epistemic status;
5. the proposed status;
6. unresolved limitations.

Do not treat additional numerical agreement as proof by itself.

## Privacy and source boundary

Do not submit private-repository content, private conversations, personal data, credentials, confidential datasets or proprietary material unless it has been explicitly cleared for public redistribution.

Public discussion should operate on the published ERP snapshot and its public evidence.

## Human–AI contributions

AI-assisted work may be disclosed as a contribution method. Any submitted scientific claim must remain attributable to a responsible human contributor who can review the exact version and respond to critique.
